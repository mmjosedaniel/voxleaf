## Product findings

- [VF-0142](../registry.md#vf-0142) **P2 · Appearance changes can settle a jump before its destination renders.** During a TOC jump to a late fragment in a large chapter, the ordinary path waits for the destination ref. A Settings appearance change starts restoration immediately. If the target is still absent 12 frames later, the ordinary settlement path accepts the unavailable result as settled, releases the reader placement gate and schedules an immediate save. Check a late fragment requiring more than 12 frames at 250 blocks per frame. Keep the gate and save pending until the ref mounts, and preserve the mounted target, relative navigation and superseded chapter controls. The input can arm intent without moving the viewport. The following programmatic scroll can satisfy the movement check while the intent is fresh. Sampling after restoration then bypasses exact-placement protection. Confirm with a real trusted wheel gesture, a settled interior offset and close/reopen. Preserve real wheel movement, same-placement restoration and viewport-only reflow cancellation.
