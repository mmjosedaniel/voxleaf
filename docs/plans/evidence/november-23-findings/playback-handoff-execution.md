## Independent review correction for VF-0169

The R01 timing calculation started the handoff clock when the main thread handled the predecessor's completion. The pre-fix worklet-to-direct fixture reported 20 ms with a 400 ms hold on the output-end delivery and a constant 300 ms semantic pause. The corrected fixture measures the delivery gap. The early direct-to-worklet fixture also lacked a valid transition before its test preparation synchronization was corrected.

The current player measures from the predecessor's estimated device output end, plus the scheduled semantic pause, to the successor's estimated device output start. Both observations must come from the same AudioContext. The worklet identifies the real end of the final quantum, including partial quanta. Endpoints reported within a single completed quantum can be projected while the UI read still sees that quantum's start. Missing, reversed and malformed endpoints withhold timing evidence while allowing valid playback to continue. [Web Audio output timestamps](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio-1.0/#dom-audiocontext-getoutputtimestamp)

Direct PCM on a timestamp-capable context schedules its source 256 frames ahead at 24 kHz, approximately 10.7 ms. That lead provides a concrete context-time boundary and contributes to measured handoff. The Pause/Resume control verifies that a resume before the scheduled frame cannot announce rendering early. Contexts without an output-clock witness proceed with direct playback and supply no timing receipt.
