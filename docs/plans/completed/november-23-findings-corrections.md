# November 23 findings corrections

## Goal

Resolve 41 source-supported cases across EPUB, reading, narration, native lifecycle and maintainer tools. Record a product disposition for the remaining case, VF-0145. ## User-visible outcome

Supported EPUBs open at stable locations with the intended direction and layout. Appearance, keyboard navigation, focus and progress remain consistent across replacement, reflow and Pause. Cancel and package actions affect the intended operation, and the next Play or retry works. Playback preserves signal tails and reports actual waits. Maintainers can distinguish measured results, absent observations and recoverable command failures.

## Risks and rollback

Maintain a reversible owner-level change for each milestone. Parser widening can alter locator identity or fail-open layout checks. Lifecycle changes can cancel a successor, strand an owner or admit work during exit. Presentation fixes can lose keyboard focus, suppress heard progress or overwrite newer preferences. Audio changes can introduce clicks, pitch drift or phantom timing. Evidence changes can pass with an absent sample or destroy valid benchmark data. If a bounded control fails, isolate or revert the associated owner change and its expectation together, preserve historical results, and rerun the affected consumers. Never repair a failing witness by relaxing a published limit.
